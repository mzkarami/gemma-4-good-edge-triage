## Objective
Reduce over-aggressive metadata-only routing on heterogeneous `event_bucket=other` slices to increase full multimodal coverage and recover quality on the 16-sample comparable benchmark while keeping latency well under 4s.

## Change Implemented
File: `triage_sandbox.py`

Added two new routing controls (default-safe):
- `TRIAGE_METADATA_SHORTCUT_OTHER_DT6_REQUIRE_SEVERE=1`
- `TRIAGE_ENABLE_METADATA_SHORTCUT_OTHER_DT5=0`

Behavioral effect:
- `other + dt6` metadata shortcut now fires only on `_severe_` events by default.
- `other + dt5` metadata shortcut is disabled by default, pushing that slice to full-MM.
- Both knobs are included in `compute_state_hash()` to preserve reproducible ledger semantics.
