# Edge-Triage Product Brief

Status: current product summary.

Edge-Triage helps disaster-response volunteers and research leads classify field reports from text and images while keeping human responders in control.

## Users

- Field volunteer / responder
- Public judge / reviewer
- Live demo operator
- Research / response lead
- Maintainer / contributor

## Product surfaces

- Static curated web demo in `site/`
- Optional guarded Live Gemma preview in `live_api.py`
- Local field CLI in `edge-triage-cli.py`
- Research sandbox in `triage_sandbox.py`

## Value

- Local-first privacy pressure for sensitive disaster data.
- Fast constrained triage categories.
- Human-readable priority and next action.
- Benchmark-driven research loop for speed/accuracy improvements.
