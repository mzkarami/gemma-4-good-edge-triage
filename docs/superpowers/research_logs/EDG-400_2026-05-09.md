## Objective

## Interpretation
- The selective escalation guard did increase full-MM usage as designed, but it did not recover confusion slices.
- On this controlled 16-sample set, quality regressed (`0.9458 -> 0.8827`) and latency increased (`224.75 ms -> 255.43 ms`).
- Misclassifications remained dominated by full-MM decisions on `dt0` variants, so routing escalation alone is not the right lever for this slice.

## Next Action
1. Keep escalation knobs defaulted OFF (current default).
2. Pivot to full-MM output stabilization for `dt0` severe/unlabelled scenes (decoder-side constraints or tie-break calibration) rather than additional route escalation.
